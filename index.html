Who We Are

Paranormal Investigations

The Whispering Spirits is a paranormal investigation group conducting organised investigations and experiences at locations with a documented history of reported paranormal activity — from private manor houses to sites of historical significance.

Our approach combines traditional investigation methods with modern equipment, careful research into each location's history, and a respectful, methodical way of working. We do not claim to prove the existence of the paranormal; we investigate, document and share what we find, and we let attendees draw their own conclusions.

  • Organised investigations at researched, historically significant locations
  • A methodical, respectful approach to every location we visit
  • Open to those new to paranormal investigation and experienced attendees alike

What To Expect

An Investigation With Us

Every evening is structured, considerate of the location, and led with care.

Researched Locations

Every site is chosen for its documented history and reported activity, with background research shared with attendees on the night.

Guided Sessions

Small-group and full-team investigation sessions led by our investigators, using a mix of traditional and equipment-based methods.

Respectful Approach

We treat every location, its history and its owners with the same respect and care we'd expect for our own homes.
